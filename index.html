Monthly Updates

  • Chattanooga search drove 67 conversions, the highest month in the 13-month lookback
  • Middle TN search conversions rose to 217 (+31) while CPC eased to $9.81 (−$0.68)
  • WBT Mergers & Acquisition display campaign ran alongside the Middle TN and Chattanooga display campaigns
  • Treasury Management ad group now live in Middle TN Business Banking

Highlights & Wins

  • Chattanooga search conversion rate reached 16.96%, up from 2.81% in March
  • HELOC drove 523 clicks in Middle TN, 79% of all Retail Banking clicks
  • Display CTR held at 0.17%, more than double the 0.05%–0.08% industry standard
  • Meta CTR of 1.61% across both markets; Chattanooga reach grew by 4,346
  • Website key events reached 659, with Google organic contributing 396

Recommendations

  • Move HELOC into its own campaign and budget so it stops pulling spend from Checking, Savings and Mortgage
  • Review Chattanooga Business Banking: $24.01 CPC with 2 conversions this month
  • Add negative keywords for low-intent loan searches (payday, tribal, no-credit-check style terms) in Middle TN Retail
  • Shift display weight from square units (0.05% CTR) toward wide banners (0.17%–0.29% CTR)

Action Items

  • HELOC campaign proposalShare the dedicated HELOC campaign recommendation with WBT for approval
  • Negative keyword passApply negatives from the flagged search terms in Middle TN Retail and MMCT
  • Business Loans reviewRevisit bids and keywords on Business Loans in both markets ($17.98 Middle TN, $28.07 Chattanooga CPC)
